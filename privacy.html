Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

FPV Tools by Saturn FPV is built as a 100% offline utility. We believe in absolute user privacy: FPV Tools collects, stores, or transmits exactly zero personal data or analytics.

This privacy policy governs your use of the software application FPV Tools ("Application") for mobile devices, which was created by Saturn FPV. The Application is a utility offering calculation tools for FPV drone pilots, including motor stator volume calculations, prop tip speed analytics, current/voltage sensor calibration, and CRSF flight telemetry log parsing.

1. What Information Does FPV Tools Collect?

None. FPV Tools does not collect, request, or harvest any personal information. You do not need to register, create an account, or sign in to use the Application.

2. Telemetry and Flight Log Data

FPV Tools allows pilots to upload and parse EdgeTX CRSF telemetry logs (.CSV files) to visualize tracks and export them as KML, KMZ, or GPX files. All parsing, math, and rendering are completed entirely locally inside the Application on your device.

  • Your flight logs are never sent to a server.
  • Your GPS coordinates and flight metrics never leave your physical device.
  • Files exported from the Application are securely shared through Android's native sharing sheet directly under your manual control.

3. Permissions Required

FPV Tools respects your device security and requests the absolute minimum system integration:

  • Internet Permission: Used solely to load online road/satellite maps inside the Telemetry Parser if your device is currently connected to the internet. If you are offline, the Application falls back to an offline tactical grid without requesting internet.
  • No Storage Permissions: The Application uses Android's secure system picker. We do not request, require, or have access to read your device's photos, media, or personal files.

4. Children's Privacy

Since the Application collects exactly zero data from any user, we do not collect or maintain any information from individuals, including children under the age of 13.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Since the Application collects no data, any policy changes will reflect minor updates to support new local offline tools or features. You are advised to consult this page for any changes.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding privacy while using the Application, or have questions about our practices, please contact us via email at info@saturnfpv.com or visit our website at saturnfpv.com.
