Software

FPV Tools

Interactive web-based tools for FPV pilots - rate conversion, PID tuning, sensor calibration, and more. All free, all open-source.

Tuning

TPA Comparison Tool

Visualize and compare Throttle PID Attenuation curves across different profiles and firmware versions.
Rates

Betaflight Rate Converter

Convert between Betaflight rate types - Actual, Betaflight, KISS, Quick Rates, and more. Visualize the curves and get a closer look at the center sensitivity.
Calibration

Current Sensor Calibration

Calculate the correct scale and offset values for your flight controller's current sensor.
Hardware

Propeller Tip Speed Calculator

Calculate tip speeds, Mach numbers, and efficiency zones for your setup. Compare theoretical performance against real-world loaded RPM.
Post-Flight

EdgeTX Telemetry Parser

Convert EdgeTX CRSF telemetry logs to KML, KMZ, or GPX. Auto-detects flights, corrects active altitude, and previews your track before exporting to Google Earth.
Hardware

Motor Stator Volume Calculator

Compare stator volumes across different motor sizes - from tiny whoops to long range builds.
Tuning

PID Controller Simulator

Interactive PID controller simulator. Visualize how P, I, and D terms respond to step inputs and disturbances in real time.
Race Day

Race Frequency Organizer

Calculate IMD-free channel assignments for multi-pilot heats. Supports Analog, HDZero, and DJI O3/O4 with automatic heat splitting and spectrum visualization.
